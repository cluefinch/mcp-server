# Code of Conduct

Cluefinch contributors are expected to keep project spaces professional, technically focused, and safe to participate in.

Be respectful of different experience levels and viewpoints. Critique code, interfaces, evidence, and proposals rather than people. Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, and publication of another person's private information are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit, hide, reject, or remove contributions and may restrict participation when behavior materially disrupts the project or violates this policy. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate to the conduct and documented privately where appropriate.

For ordinary disagreements, use the relevant issue or pull request and keep the discussion tied to reproducible behavior and project scope. For sensitive conduct reports, contact the maintainers privately at hello@cluefinch.com rather than opening a public issue.
